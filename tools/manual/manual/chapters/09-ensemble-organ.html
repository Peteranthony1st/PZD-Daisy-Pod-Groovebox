Ensemble and Organ

Two engines share one set of pages. The Ensemble is a divide-down string machine in the ARP Solina tradition: seven drawbars of saws and squares, a built-in stereo chorus, an LFO and velocity, with 19 factory sounds. The Organ is a tonewheel-style drawbar organ: nine drawbars of pure sines in Hammond order, with percussion and key click, and eight classic registrations. Open them from Global:Ensmbl and Global:Organ.

Registration

K1 picks a drawbar and K2 sets its level; the bar's footage shows at the top. The Organ's run 16', 5 1/3', 8', 4', 2 2/3', 2', 1 3/5', 1 1/3' and 1'. Both start with every drawbar at 60%.

The Ensemble's LFO and velocity

The LFO moves the pitch of each note (with Fade, so vibrato comes in after the note starts), the filter (when its type isn't Off), or the level: a Square synced to 1/16 is a trance gate. Synced, it stays on the beat with the looper. The LFODep page sets how much it moves each one; either side of centre moves it the opposite way.

Velocity (on the MIDI page, B2 then K1): at 0 every note plays alike, as on a string machine. Turned up, softer notes are quieter, darker and slower to swell; harder ones play the registration as set, with a snappier attack.

The Organ's percussion and click

Percussion is single-trigger, as on a tonewheel organ: one shared ping restarts only when you press a key with no other key held, and every note sounding while it fades carries it. So a struck chord pings on every note, and legato lines have none. Key click adds the contact click at each note's start.

The Organ has no LFO pages and doesn't respond to velocity. Its User saves go in ORGAN/.
