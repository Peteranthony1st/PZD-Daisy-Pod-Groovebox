Saving, export and the card

Nothing saves by itself: everything reaches the card when you save it. There are three kinds of save, plus each engine's own User saves (Chapter 7).

Performances

On Global:Perf, tap B1 to save: K1 picks Overwrite (the Performance loaded) or Save New, and holding B2 confirms. To load, tap B2 to open the list, turn K2 and tap B2. The list starts with New (blank). A * after the title means something has changed since the last load or save.

The card template's PERF001 is the demo song.

Snapshots

Global:Snap saves the same way: B1, K1 for Overwrite or Save New, hold B2. B2 opens the list, K2 scrolls and B2 loads. The page lists the engines in two columns, each with its on/off box (filled = on) and its sound's name: what's live now, or while browsing, what the highlighted snapshot holds. Grains' audio is saved in the snapshot; the samplers' sounds and kits are saved by name.

Export

Global:Export writes a WAV to the card. K1 picks what:

  • Audio Loop: one loop of the audio tracks, rendered in a few seconds and normalised. No MIDI tracks.
  • Audio+MIDI Loop: everything you hear except the click, recorded live from bar 1 for 1 to 8 loops (K2), up to about 38 seconds.
  • Song: the song, from the step picked on the Song page to its end (Chapter 5).

Tap B1 for a full-quality 48 kHz file, or B2 for 44.1 kHz, saved straight into a custom/ folder so the same card works in a MicroDexed Touch's second slot. Tap either again to stop. In the two live modes the master volume sets the level, with the limiter catching peaks.

Managing files on the card

Global:SD Mgmt copies and deletes files without a computer. It opens on a grid of the eight kinds of save: Global (Performances or Snapshots), Grains and Dexed User saves, SYNTH (the Ensemble's, Organ's or Supersaw's), Captures, Sampler imports, and the SMPVO and SMPDR User saves.

K1 moves around the grid and folders and B2 opens. In a file list, K2 scrolls; B1 goes back, or held, duplicates the file; holding B2 for 1.5 seconds deletes it.
