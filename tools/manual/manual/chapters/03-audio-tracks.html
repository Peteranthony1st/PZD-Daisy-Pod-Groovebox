Audio tracks

The four audio tracks, A1–A4, are the looper. Each records the audio input (and any engine you play while it records) into a loop that all four share, locked to one tempo. The loop's length is set in bars on Global:Tempo.

Recording a loop

  1. On Home, turn to the track you want, A1–A4.
  2. Hold B1. If the metronome is on, it counts in one bar first, with LED 1 flashing blue.
  3. Play. LED 2 is red while it records, and it stops by itself at the end of the loop. Tap B1 to stop early.

Then:

On HomeWhat happens
Tap B1Pause or play the selected track.
Hold B1Overdub: record more on top of the track, from the next count-in.
K1Master volume.
K2Metronome volume.
B2Bypass on or off: hear the input without recording it.
Engines record too. Anything you play on an engine while a track records goes into the take, mixed with the input. No cable needed.

A track's pages

Click the encoder on a selected track to open its pages, and turn to move between them. Click again, or hold, to go back.

Status

The track's waveform, with a moving playhead. K1 is its volume and K2 its pan. B1 records, pauses and overdubs, as on Home. Tap B2 to undo the last overdub; hold it for about a second to clear the track.

Gain

K1 sets the input gain, 1× to 4×. While this page is open you hear the input through it, and its level shows against the clip line: set it as high as it goes without touching the line.

Filter

B1 picks the filter: Off, low-pass, high-pass or band-pass. K1 is the cutoff and K2 the resonance. The page draws the filter's response.

Drive

K1 drives the track into saturation (0 is off). K2 trims the level back, with the middle leaving it as it is. The page draws the driven wave.

Tremolo

K1 is the depth (0 is off) and K2 the rate, 0.5 to 10 Hz. The page draws the level over time.

LYR-FX

K1 sends the track to the shared reverb and K2 to the shared delay. The page draws both, small.

Tempo and the loop's length

On Global:Tempo, K1 sets the tempo, 50 to 240 BPM, and K2 the loop's length, 1 to 16 bars. At slow tempos the longest loops aren't available, because a loop has a fixed amount of memory. B1 switches the metronome on and off. Holding B2 saves the power-on defaults: the tempo and loop length, with the master volume, Bypass, the master filter, the reverb and the delay.

The page shows the BPM large, a metronome whose arm swings on each beat while the click is on, a box for each beat, and a box for each bar of the loop. The bar playing is filled; dotted bars are ones you could still add. While any track holds audio, the page shows LOCKED: the tempo and length are fixed until the tracks are cleared.

Switching the looper off

Global:Looper switches all four tracks on or off with B1, freeing their share of the processor for the engines. B2 sets whether the looper is on at power-on, and clicking the encoder there opens Home.
