Mixer and effects

Everything meets in the mixer, then the shared effects: one plate reverb and one stereo delay that every track and engine sends into, and a master filter. A soft limiter on the master output catches loud peaks instead of clipping.

Global:Tracks and Global:Synths

The first two Global pages are the mixer. Tracks shows the four audio tracks and the eight MIDI tracks (L1–L4, M1–M8); Synths shows the eight engines. Both end with Bypass (the monitored input) and Master. Each channel is a name over a live peak meter, from −48 dB to 0 dBFS, with a clip mark.

On both, K1 is the Master Vol and K2 the Bypass Vol, so you can pull a live mic down without touching the mix (Bypass starts at 60%). The bottom row is the live processor load, average and peak; tap B1 for a breakdown by part.

A MIDI track has no sound of its own, so its channel is its engine's: M1 playing DXD-A shows and sets DXD-A's level, and turning one turns the other.

A channel's settings

Click the encoder to step into the channels one by one; turn to move between them, and past the last for a live oscilloscope of the final mix. On a channel, K1 is its volume and K2 its pan. B2 (Rev/Dly) turns them into its reverb send (K1) and delay send (K2), and B1 brings back volume and pan. On Master, K1 is the volume and K2 the reverb's size. A MIDI track's channel is titled with its engine, like M1>DXA.

Every channel pans with an equal-power law, −3 dB in the centre.

Global:Sends

Every channel's reverb and delay send on one page: the audio tracks, each engine (DXD-A and DXD-B share the Dexed bus's) and Bypass, each with two bars. K1 picks the channel; B1 points K2 at its reverb send and B2 at its delay send.

The delay

One shared stereo delay, with free time or a note value synced to the tempo, ping-pong, a tone that darkens each repeat, and a send that puts its echoes into the reverb. Its pages draw an echo timeline.

The timeline's spacing is Time, shrinking by Feedback, with ping-pong above and below the line, thinner repeats for a darker Tone, and a haze after each for Dly-Rev. Beat ticks show when it's synced. At 0% Ping-Pong the echoes follow the sound's pan; at 100% they always bounce left and right.

The master filter and Bypass

Global:Filter filters the whole mix: K1 cutoff, K2 resonance, B1 the type (Off, low-pass, high-pass, band-pass).

Global:Bypass sets the monitored input's own reverb and delay sends, with the same scope and meter as an engine's Mix page. Switch Bypass on with B2 on Home.

Global:Speed

Vari-speed for the whole project: K1 changes the speed and pitch of everything together, and B2 resets it to 1.0×. B1 switches the transport between Normal, Scrub and Freeze. In Scrub, the encoder moves through the loop like a tape; in Freeze, it nudges the frozen spot. Hold the encoder to leave.
