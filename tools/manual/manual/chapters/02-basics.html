Finding your way

Every screen on the Groovebox is laid out the same way, and the same few moves get you anywhere. This chapter covers them once, so the rest of the manual can just name the page and the control.

The screen

A small * after a page's title means its sound has changed since it was last loaded or saved.

Turning, clicking and holding

EncoderWhat it does
TurnOn Home, moves through the tracks, then the Scenes and Song pages. Anywhere else, moves through that area's pages.
ClickGoes one level in, or back out. From Home it opens the selected track. On a Global page with PUSH ENC TO OPEN, it opens that engine or mixer, and clicking again comes back.
HoldFrom Home, opens Global. From anywhere else, goes back Home.
Triple-clickThree clicks within about a second: All Notes Off on every engine, for a stuck note. You stay on the page you were on.

Home

Home is where you start and where you come back to. Turning the encoder there moves through the four audio tracks (A1–A4), the eight MIDI tracks (M1–M8), then the Scenes and Song pages. The tracks show four to a page, each box with its state and a playhead along the bottom.

On Home, K1 is the master volume and K2 the metronome's. B1 records, pauses and overdubs the selected track, and B2 switches Bypass, which lets you hear the input. Each box shows its track's state:

Global

Hold the encoder on Home to open Global, the settings that aren't any one track's. Turn through its pages; hold again to go back Home.

Knobs pick up

The knobs don't jump the moment you touch them. Each value on the screen is the setting as it is now; turning a knob only takes control once it reaches that value. So moving to a new page never changes anything until you mean it to.

The screen saver

After a few minutes with nothing touched, the screen dims, then switches off a minute later. The first touch only wakes it, so it can't change anything by accident. Set the time on Global:Display: Off, 1, 2, 5, 10 or 30 minutes (5 to start with). B1 there puts it to sleep now, and B2 saves the time for every power-on.
