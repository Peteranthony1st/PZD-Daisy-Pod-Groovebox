The samplers

Two sampler engines share most of their pages. SMPVO (Sample Sound) plays one sound across the keyboard: a single sample, a multi-sample, or a capture. SMPDR (Sample Drum) plays a kit or a break, one sample per key from C2. Open them from Global:SMPVO and Global:SMPDR.

Velocity starts at 50% on SMPVO and at 0% on SMPDR, where every hit plays at full level until you turn it up. It's kept with each User save.

SMPVO: one sound across the keyboard

SMPVO's Preset page starts with Sounds, Captured and Multi, then a folder, then an item. Its own pages capture and tune a sound:

Capture: B1 picks the source (Direct, Layer or Import) and holding B2 records or captures. Trim: K1 and K2 set the start and end, B1 plays the result and holding B2 saves it as a Sound. Tune: K1 tunes captures and K2 imports, ±24 semitones, and B1 and B2 switch each between Mono and Poly.

SMPDR: kits and breaks

SMPDR's Preset page goes straight to its Kits and Breaks. A kit plays its pads poly; a break starts with Choke on, so each new pad cuts the last.

Setup: K1 tunes the kit, ±24 semitones. B1 switches Mono (choke) and Poly for this kit. K2 sets how many slices to cut a single loop into, 0–16, and holding B2 applies it.

Sounds, kits and breaks from a computer

The sample library lives in numbered folders of up to 32 items under SAMPLES/ on the card: SOUNDS1, CAPTURED1, MULTISMP1, KITS1, BREAKS1 and so on. When one fills, the next is made. You can add to it on a computer, no import needed:

  • Sounds: copy .wav files into a SAMPLES/SOUNDS folder. Any sample rate from 8 to 192 kHz, 16 or 24-bit, 32-bit or float, mono or stereo. A note at the end of the name, like Cello C3.wav, sets its root.
  • Multi-samples: make a folder in a MULTISMP folder with one WAV per recorded note, the note in each name. Each key plays from the nearest recording. Up to 24 notes and about 33 seconds.
  • Kits and breaks: make a folder in a KITS or BREAKS folder and copy WAVs in. They load in name order, one per key from C2 (01 kick.wav, 02 snare.wav…). Up to 24 pads and about 33 seconds.

Importing OP-1 packs

OP-1 sample packs, as .aif files from op1.fun or the OP-1 pack builder, import directly, with their slice points and pitch:

  1. Copy the files into the staging folders at the card's root: DRUM PACK IMPORT/KITS/ for drum packs, DRUM PACK IMPORT/BREAKS/ for breaks and loops, and SOUND PACK IMPORT/ for sampler patches.
  2. On SMPDR's or SMPVO's Import page (on SMPDR, K1 picks Kits or Breaks and B2 opens it), K1 picks the destination: a new bank, or an existing one with room. Hold B2 to import every file in the folder.
  3. Imported files move into the folder's Done/. A file that fails stays for the next try.

op1.fun often names downloads patch.aif: rename it first, to give the kit a proper name.
