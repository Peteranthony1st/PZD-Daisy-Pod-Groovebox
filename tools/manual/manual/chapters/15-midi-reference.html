MIDI and reference

Global:MIDI

Every engine's MIDI channel in one place, one column each: GR, DXA, DXB, ENS, ORG, SSW, SMV and SMD, each with its on/off dot and channel. K1 picks an engine and K2 sets its channel, Omni or 1–16: the same setting as the engine's own MIDI page. Hold B2 to save every channel as its power-on default.

While DXD-B is locked to DXD-A it shows =A; giving it a different channel here unlocks it.

The sustain pedal

A pedal sending CC 64 holds notes on every engine, each on its own MIDI channel. Letting go releases every held note except keys still down, and playing a held note again restarts it. Sustained notes count towards an engine's voices.

If your pedal works backwards (some keyboards detect the pedal at power-on), tap B1 on Global:MIDI to switch between Sus-Pedal:Norm and Sus-Pedal:Rev; it's saved straight away. A SUS box in the page's title shows the pedal live, filled while it's down.

The mod wheel

The Ensemble, Organ and Supersaw each pick what the mod wheel moves, with B1 on their MIDI page: the Ensemble and Organ Off, Chorus, Level or Filter; the Supersaw Off, Detune, Cutoff or Level.

Stuck notes

Triple-click the encoder, or send CC 123 (All Notes Off): every engine lets go of every note, sustained ones too. ALL NOTES OFF shows briefly.

Keeping the processor in hand

The Groovebox runs at 480 MHz, and every engine skips its work while it's silent, so an engine that's on but not playing costs almost nothing. With every engine playing chords over four looping tracks with effects, the load peaks in the mid-80s. To keep headroom:

  • Switch off engines a song doesn't use: off frees their share entirely.
  • Lower an engine's Voices on its MIDI page, especially Dexed's, when several engines play. The Organ is the cheapest of the chord engines.
  • Record synth parts into audio tracks, then switch those engines off.
  • Set the reverb and delay last: several engines sending into the effects at once is the heaviest load.
  • A track's Drive and Tremolo cost nothing at 0.

The bottom row of Global:Tracks and Global:Synths shows the load live; tap B1 there for a breakdown: Grains, Dexed, Ensemble, Organ, SMPVO, SMPDR, Supersaw, the looper, the delay, the reverb and everything else, each as average and peak.
