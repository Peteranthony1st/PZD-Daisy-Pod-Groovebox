SampleSynth Drum (SMPDR) Kits -- one folder per kit, each holding its
KIT.DAT (pad mapping + settings) and its own pad sounds (01.wav,
02.wav, ...).

Kits are made by importing OP-1 drum packs (DRUM PACK IMPORT/): each
pack becomes a folder named after the pack. Up to 32 kits per KITSn
folder; once this one is full, new kits go into KITS2/, KITS3/, ... --
created automatically.

Browse them from Screen::SampleSynth (Drum) -> Preset page: pick a
folder ("Kits 1"), then a kit. Delete/Duplicate from Global:SdMgmt ->
KITS. Deleting a kit removes its whole folder.
